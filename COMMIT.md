**In short:** your pet notices when you've been farming the same thing for an hour, and wishes and weather repeat themselves less.

- An hour of farming rep, gold, herbs, ore, skins, fish or quests with your pet out, liked or not, gets a line from it now and then
- An hour of killing the same kind of mobs gets a line naming them by the word they share, like "Deadwood"
- Your pet never wishes for the same thing two days in a row
- Logging in while it rains or snows no longer has your pet say it's starting to
- After a long break the same day, your pet can tell you again what it enjoyed doing with you
