_Developed with the help of Claude (Anthropic)._

**0.10.0 is the content release.** Your pets have far more to say, and they notice more of the world: the weather,
your race and class, the other faction, the great bosses and dungeons. Having many pets pays off now: Turnus takes
them out in turn, the card tells you when to swap, families you bond with earn a badge, and a new Flokk tab and
achievements follow your whole herd.

### Many pets
- Turnus: pick two to five pets with the small button on the corner of "Med deg", and they take turns being out with you, a new one each day and the next when one's day is full
- When your pet's day is full, the card of a pet with room today says "Bytt til" in green, and the chat line names the pet that has waited longest: click it to take that pet out
- The pet frame shows the day in the name once it's full: a gold check, or green arrows when another pet still has room
- A family badge on the pet card: bonds at level 3 with three, five or every pet of a family earn a bronze, silver or gold ring with the family's mark
- A "Flokk" tab beside a pet's story: your Turnus, and everything you've been through with all your pets, newest first
- Pets of yours with a bond get to know each other, and a long time apart doesn't go unnoticed

### The world
- Pets notice the weather: rain, snow and sandstorms where you are, and some pets like or dislike them (frogs and Vannhund pets love rain, Vinterpels snow, snakes and reptiles a sandstorm, and a Solelsker minds rain and snow)
- Every race's racials get a reaction from your pet: a Dwarf's Stoneform, a Gnome's Escape Artist, an Orc's Blood Fury, the Skyborne's Walk on Air and more
- More for pets to react to in five classes: a warrior's Shield Wall, a rogue's Vanish, a warlock's Eye of Kilrogg, a priest's Psychic Scream, a paladin's Lay on Hands and more
- Pets notice when you walk into the other faction's capital
- The big bosses and dungeons get lines of their own the first time a pet is there: VanCleef, Ragnaros, Onyxia, the Deadmines, Molten Core and more
- A pet's first visit to an iconic zone gets its own line, by its family where it fits: a frog in Swamp of Sorrows, a mechanical pet in Gnomeregan, a wolf in Winterspring
- A Tivolidyr pet loves the Darkmoon Faire: walk into it on a faire day and it tells you

### More to say
- Over a hundred new things for pets to say: when they do what they like, by the time of day and the season, a guildmate's pat, a new boss, a wish come true, the holidays and their traits' moments
- A pet never says the same line twice in a row
- Pets with a personality of their own say more different things: their own lines now mix with everyone's, and a shaman's resurrect and a warlock's Inferno get a reaction only now and then

### Achievements
- Bestevenn and Sjelevenn with your pet, the rodents, wolves, bugs and holiday pets, playdates, pats, cuddles, firsts and wishes, and a new section, Flokken, for bonds with many pets and Turnusvakt

### Balance
- Back after a week or more away, your pet has missed you: the first pet out that day gets its day's points twice
- A pet you haven't adopted gets its day's points once it has been out with you for ten minutes, not the moment it's summoned
- Likes that cost nothing, a jump, a line in guild chat, a dance or a hug, give their points once a minute at most; the emotes waited five minutes before
- A pet gets used to what it dislikes: after the tenth time it says so, and from then on it complains once a week at most
