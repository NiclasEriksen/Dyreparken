- Sixteen reactions have a third line: totems, Astral Recall, Vanish, Shield Wall, Shadowform and more
- Your pet answers when you /bonk, /boop or /pat it, at most once a minute for each
- The card's bond bar shows two shades again: today's points reach the bar's tip instead of stopping short
- When a pet's day is full, the suggested pet to swap to is the next in your Turnus with room, before any other
- Once the Stranglethorn fishing contest is over (its end from the game's calendar), a pet no longer talks about it, and fishing there gives no contest bonus
- The pet frame's background fills its box without black bars, cut to its shape as on the card
