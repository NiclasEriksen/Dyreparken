_Developed with the help of Claude (Anthropic)._

**In short:** your pet meets the faction leaders, has more to say and more to wish for, guildmates' pets tell more on hover, and auto-summon no longer trips over chairs, crafting or loading screens. Plus a long list of fixes from a full playtest review.

- Your pet meets your faction's leaders: its own words the first time it sees Thrall, Cairne, Magni and the others, and bond points; a new home inn now and then gets a line too
- More words for pets that had one line for a moment: a druid's cat form, Sprint, Pick Pocket, Inner Fire and others
- Pets with only one or two things to wish for get more kinds of wishes: their unlocked minor like, their family's likes, and a few everyday ones
- Your adopted pet tells you today's wish in chat, once a day, a minute after it first comes out
- A guildmate's pet they haven't adopted shows their bond with it, and its traits, when you hover it in the world
- A guildmate's adopted pet that may wish for a pat today says so when you hover it, in the world and on Parken
- Playdates happen when a group formed far apart meets up: your pet looks for playmates again at each new place while you're grouped
- Turnus says when it stands still («Med deg» off) and dims its badge, and its texts tell that your adopted pet goes first each day
- The card's "?" shows each pet's own pace: half for a pet you haven't adopted, and how to get full
- Your pet's bond lines come one at a time, a few seconds apart, instead of several in the same second at a boss kill
- At a boss kill, your pet's daily line about what it loves no longer comes in the same breath as a first-time line; it waits for a later moment
- The setting "Skjul ukjente dyr" is now called "Ukjente dyr som spørsmålstegn", which is what it does
- A guild member with no pets at all is welcomed with where pets come from, not told to adopt one
- The welcome for a new player comes a minute after logging in, out of a fight, instead of among the login's own messages
- The "fornøyd for i dag" nudge toward another pet goes quiet after three days of it unheeded, and comes back once you swap pets
- Hopping about before stepping off a small ledge no longer gives the big-fall line
- Auto-summon no longer calls twice in a row or into a hearth's loading screen, which could send your pet home
- «Litt» in Småting i chatten now also halves the daily line about something your pet loves
- When your pet's day is full, a pet that was out earlier today with room left is suggested too
- Playdates count for both pets when one owner's pet comes out just after the other's announced
- A pet you haven't adopted no longer loses its day's visit when a flight path or loading screen takes it away at the wrong moment
- A faction leader met on a character with no guild is saved for when you have one
- A dungeon's named end boss always gets its line now, even after a day of other firsts
- A caged or sold adopted pet no longer stops the Turnus: the others take their turns
- Turnus works on a character with no «Med deg» pet yet: its pick of the day comes out
- A pet you take out of the Turnus is no longer brought out or suggested that same day
- Auto-summon waits while a profession window is open, so it no longer stops your crafting queue
- Auto-summon waits while you sit, so swapping pets in a chair no longer sends your pet away
- A small fall right after a big one no longer gets a second line from your pet
- Dagens dyr stays put through the day: someone adopting or letting go of a pet no longer moves the crown
