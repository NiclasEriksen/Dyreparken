_Developed with the help of Claude (Anthropic)._

- Weather on a flight path no longer counts for a pet that isn't out; landing in the rain does
- Over a hundred new things for pets to say: when they do what they like, by the time of day and the season, a guildmate's pat, a new boss, a wish come true, the holidays and their traits' moments
- Some pets like or dislike the weather now: frogs and Vannhund pets love rain, Vinterpels snow, snakes and reptiles a sandstorm, and a Solelsker minds rain and snow
- Pets notice the weather: when ***** starts where you are, your pet may tell you what it thinks of it, by its nature
- New achievements: Bestevenn and Sjelevenn with your pet, the rodents, wolves, bugs and holiday pets, playdates, pats, cuddles, firsts and wishes, and a new page section, Flokken, for bonds with many pets and Turnusvakt
- Turnus: pick two to five pets with the small button beside "Med deg", and they take turns being out with you, a new one each day and the next when one's day is full
- The pet frame's day mark sits in the name now, small, and only once the day is full: a gold check, or green arrows when another pet still has room
- A "Flokk" tab beside a pet's story: everything you've been through with all your pets, newest first
- A family badge on the pet card: bonds at level 3 with three, five or every pet of a family earn a bronze, silver or gold ring with the family's mark
- Pets of yours with a bond get to know each other: *****, and *****
- The pet frame shows the day beside the name: a ring while it has room, a gold check when it's full, and green arrows when another of your pets still has room
- When your pet's day is full, the card of a pet with room today says "Bytt inn" in green, and the chat line's pet is a link: click it to take that pet out
- When your pet's day is full, it tells you which of your other pets would like to come along: the one that has waited longest
- Back after a week or more away, your pet has missed you: the first pet out that day gets its day's points twice
- A pet gets used to what it dislikes: after the tenth time it says so, and from then on it complains once a week at most
- Likes that cost nothing, a jump, a line in guild chat, a dance or a hug, give their points once a minute at most; the emotes waited five minutes before
- A pet you haven't adopted gets its day's points once it has been out with you for ten minutes, not the moment it's summoned
- A pet never says the same line twice in a row: with more than one way to say something, it picks another than last time
- Pets with a personality of their own say more different things: their own lines now mix with everyone's, and a shaman's resurrect and a warlock's Inferno get a reaction only now and then
